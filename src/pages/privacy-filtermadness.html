Privacy Policy for FilterMadness

Last updated: April 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use the FilterMadness app ("the App").

Information We Collect

Photo Access

FilterMadness accesses your photo library only with your explicit permission. We request access to apply artistic filters to your selected photos and to save the filtered results back to your photo library.

Photo Processing

When you apply filters to photos, the selected images are processed using OpenAI's image editing API. This means your photos are transmitted to OpenAI's servers for processing. This transmission is necessary to provide the core functionality of the app. The processing is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

FilterMadness stores your filtered images locally on your device. We maintain a record of your purchased credits in your device's local storage. No images or personal data are stored on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

  • Process your images with artistic filters
  • Maintain your credit balance for in-app purchases
  • Provide app functionality

Third-Party Services

OpenAI API

We use OpenAI's image processing API to apply filters to your photos. Your images are sent to OpenAI for processing, but we do not store your images on our servers. Please refer to OpenAI's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle data.

Apple App Store

All purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect, store, or process any payment information. Apple's handling of payment information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous usage data to improve app performance and user experience. This data cannot be used to identify you personally and is used solely for app improvement purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy (specifically, the transmission to OpenAI for image processing).

Children's Privacy

FilterMadness is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

To delete all data associated with FilterMadness, simply delete the app from your device. This will remove all locally stored information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@getaskjoe.com
